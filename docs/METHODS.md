# Methods

Replace the prompts below with the exact methods used to generate the published data.

## Molecular modelling
Document surface-model construction, software and version, electronic-structure method or force field, basis set, charge, spin, solvation, optimisation criteria, temperature, starting configurations, corrections and uncertainty assessment. Define the interaction-energy equation and sign convention.

## Soil and environmental chemistry
Document material preparation, pH, ionic strength, solid-to-liquid ratio, nutrient concentrations, equilibration time, temperature, analytical methods, calibrations, blanks, controls, replication and mass-balance checks.

## Granule formulation and characterisation
Document feedstock, pyrolysis conditions, pre-treatment, particle size, nutrient source, binder, moisture content, mixing and granulation parameters. Report methods for size distribution, crushing strength, attrition, bulk density, water uptake, disintegration and nutrient release.

## Crop evaluation
Document crop variety, soil source and properties, experimental design, randomisation, replication, fertiliser rate, growth conditions, irrigation, harvest timing, measured outcomes and statistical analysis.

## Decision model
Define descriptor normalisation, directionality, missing-data handling, weighting, aggregation, threshold rules and sensitivity analysis. State clearly whether the score is exploratory, screening-level or validated for a specified use.

## Reproducibility
Record software versions, analysis scripts, random seeds, data exclusions, transformations, quality-control status and repository release identifier.
